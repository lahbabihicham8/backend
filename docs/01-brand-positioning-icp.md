# Brand Positioning, ICP, and Copy Rules

## Brand

Arabic: `خفيفة`

English: `khafeefa`

Meaning: light, easy, effortless. The name should feel soft, practical, and premium, not childish.

Positioning sentence:

`خفيفة` is a Kuwait-first DTC comfort brand that curates practical products for hot weather, long errands, family outings, and daily movement.

Brand promise:

`نختار لك منتجات تخفف يومك، ونختبرها على واقع الخليج قبل ما نوصلها لك.`

Do not position the store as a marketplace or gadget shop. Position it as a focused branded store with selected products under one quality standard.

## ICP

Primary buyer:

- Kuwait women and men, 22-45.
- Arabic-first, social commerce buyers.
- Used to Snapchat/TikTok discovery.
- Comfortable with COD.
- Trusts brands that show proof, reviews, faces, clear policies, and fast local support.
- Wants convenience, comfort, and a clean-looking product without feeling scammed by cheap random stores.

High-intent subgroups:

- Women who go out often, drive, shop, work, or move between family visits.
- Men who work outdoors, walk between parking and offices, go camping/chalets, or want practical gadgets.
- Parents buying for kids, mothers, drivers, helpers, or older family members.
- Gift buyers who want a useful product that looks thoughtful.

## Emotional Hooks

Use these emotional themes across ads and pages:

- Kuwait heat is exhausting even during simple errands.
- Carrying many devices is annoying. One compact product feels smarter.
- Looking sweaty or uncomfortable in public is embarrassing.
- People want to feel prepared in the car, mall, chalet, office, school pickup, or outdoor waiting.
- Buying one cheap generic item often means weak battery, noisy fan, bad plastic, unclear support, and no one to contact.
- COD reduces fear, but brand proof closes the sale.

## Authority Hooks

Use proof language, not fake medical claims:

- Air movement helps sweat evaporate and supports perceived cooling.
- Hands-free airflow is more practical than handheld fans during errands and walking.
- Multi-use design reduces the number of items customers carry.
- Battery/power bank feature is positioned as emergency convenience, not a replacement for a full-size power bank.
- Quality checks: airflow, battery charging, clip/strap comfort, button response, packaging condition.

Forbidden:

- Do not claim medical treatment.
- Do not claim official government certification unless real certificates are provided.
- Do not invent customer reviews as real. Use labeled mock reviews until real reviews are available.
- Do not say "original" unless the brand owns manufacturing/IP proof. Say "Khafeefa selected", "Khafeefa checked", or "Khafeefa edition".

## Copy Tone

Use GCC/Kuwait-friendly Arabic. Since the user asked for KSA dialect too, keep language understandable across Gulf Arabic, but prioritize Kuwait purchase trust.

Tone:

- Warm, direct, premium, practical.
- Short sentences.
- Use Arabic numerals only when clearer for prices.
- Avoid overhyped scam words like "معجزة" and "لن تصدق".
- Use high-trust CTA labels.

Good phrases:

- `اطلبها الآن والدفع عند الاستلام`
- `توصيل داخل الكويت`
- `اختبرناها للحر والطلعات اليومية`
- `خفيفة على الخصر، قوية وقت تحتاجها`
- `قطعة وحدة للهواء والشحن`
- `مناسبة للدوام، السيارة، السوق، الشاليه والمشاوير`
- `وفر أكثر مع باقة العائلة`

Avoid:

- `أرخص سعر`
- `دروبشيبنق`
- `مضمون 100% يعالج`
- `شحن عالمي`
- `الكمية خرافية`

## Product Naming

Product page title:

`مروحة خفيفة للخصر مع باور بانك`

Subtitle:

`هواء قريب منك + شحن للطوارئ في جهاز واحد، مصمم لمشاوير الكويت والحر اليومي.`

Short card heading:

`مروحة خصر وباور بانك`

Short card subheading:

`خفيفة، عملية، وتخليك جاهز للحر والمشاوير.`

## Social Proof Rules

Until real UGC exists, use placeholders clearly marked in code/content as demo:

- `+1,200 طلب تجريبي/placeholder` must not be shown as real.
- Show UI slots for video reviews, star rating, and before/after comfort stories.
- Admin/content file should allow replacing mock reviews with real reviews later.

Real proof to collect:

- Name initials and area: `ن. أ. - السالمية`
- Rating.
- Product photo/video.
- Delivery confirmation.
- Customer objection solved: airflow, battery, comfort, COD, delivery.

## Trust Badges

Use these as store guarantees:

- `الدفع عند الاستلام`
- `فحص قبل التغليف`
- `توصيل داخل الكويت`
- `استبدال خلال 7 أيام عند وجود عيب مصنعي`
- `دعم واتساب`
- `بياناتك تستخدم لتأكيد الطلب فقط`
